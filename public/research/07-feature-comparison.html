1. Summary

  • 7 mechanics are the same across operators, 8 similar, 9 unique.
  • Everyone shares the core: a weighted odds table with independent opens, published odds, a sell-back into balance.
  • The case sites (Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty) form one family: committed secret, player seed, verifier. Evo Verse, IcyBox, StockX and Courtyard have no proof.
  • Battles and the upgrader appear on Evo Verse, Jemlit and Cases.gg only.
  • The unique mechanics are where margin or honesty lives: Misty's credit-only payouts, free spins and frozen table; IcyBox's early retirement; StockX's tiers; Courtyard's price-from-contents; our market value.
  • Evo Verse has 8 of 24 mechanics: 5 shared, 3 similar, 0 unique. Its gap is the proof family.

3. Same, similar, unique

Same across operators (three or more have it)

  • Sell an item back for balance. Convert a won item into balance instead of shipping it. Found at: Evo Verse, IcyBox, StockX (~), Courtyard, Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty (~).
  • Exact odds published per outcome. The player can see the chance of every row before paying. Found at: Evo Verse (~), IcyBox (~), StockX, Courtyard (~), Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty.
  • Weighted odds table, independent opens. Every open is its own roll against a fixed table; nothing is remembered between opens. Found at: Evo Verse, StockX (~), Courtyard (~), Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty.
  • Secret committed before the open. A server seed is chosen and its hash shown before payment; the seed is revealed after. Found at: Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty.
  • Player sets a seed. A word of the player's goes into the roll so the site cannot pick it alone. Found at: Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty.
  • Case battles. Several players open the same boxes; the highest total takes all. Found at: Evo Verse, Jemlit, Cases.gg.
  • Upgrader or Deals. Stake money or an item for a chance at a better item. Found at: Evo Verse, Jemlit, Cases.gg.

Similar (two or more, with variants)

  • Public page that recomputes an open. Anyone can paste the seeds and get the same result. Found at: StockX (~), Courtyard (~), Jemlit, Cases.gg, Misty (~).
  • Missions, streaks, free box, spend caps. Mechanics that bring the player back or slow them down. Found at: Evo Verse, IcyBox (~), Jemlit (~), Cases.gg (~), Misty (~).
  • Money leaves only as shipped items. No cash withdrawal; balance turns into money only by shipping an item. Found at: Evo Verse, Jemlit (~), Cases.gg (~), Misty.
  • Most opens pay site credit. Losing opens hand back credit that only buys more boxes. Found at: Evo Verse (~), Jemlit (~), Cases.gg (~), Misty.
  • Fresh secret for every open. The seed changes every open, so a revealed seed never explains another open. Found at: Jemlit (~), Cases.gg (~), Misty.
  • Collectible card packs. Boxes whose items are trading cards; the same draw as a box. Found at: Courtyard, Jemlit.
  • Finite pool, drawn without replacement. A fixed stock of copies; each win removes one; the box can run out. Found at: IcyBox, StockX (~).
  • Independent market value shown. A value next to the operator's own figure. Found at: StockX (~), Courtyard (~).

Unique to one operator

  • Tier first, then an item inside the tier. The roll picks a tier; the item inside comes from inventory. Found at: StockX.
  • Operator may retire a finite box early. A pool box can be withdrawn before its top prize has been drawn. Found at: IcyBox.
  • Odds table hashed and frozen onto the open. The check shows which table the roll was read against. Found at: Misty.
  • Bonus spins inside an open. Some outcomes trigger extra rolls on a small credit table. Found at: Misty.
  • Crash game. A multiplier climbs until it crashes; cash out before it does. Found at: Jemlit.
  • Mines game. Reveal tiles on a grid, avoid the mines, cash out before you hit one. Found at: Jemlit.
  • Borrowing against play. The site lends balance to keep a player playing. Found at: Jemlit.
  • Prize competitions with a free postal entry. Numbered entries, a draw date, a free route at equal odds. Found at: Misty.
  • Price derived from the contents. The price is computed from what is inside, not fixed first. Found at: Courtyard.

6. Takeaways for our build

  • Table stakes: weighted table, published odds, sell-back.
  • Proof is the dividing line: three operators commit a secret, four do not. Per-open commitment with a frozen table (Misty, Glass Box) is the strongest version and costs nothing to run.
  • Credit-only payouts are Misty's margin engine and unique. Copy the mechanism only with a published rate.
  • Battles and upgrader are expected on a case site and absent on luxury boxes; Evo Verse already ships both.
  • Nobody shows an independent market value except our design: the cheapest visible difference.
  • Price from contents (Courtyard, Glass Box) is rare and honest; price first is the industry habit.

Glossary

Mechanic
One rule of the product that can be present or absent on its own: a draw model, a proof step, a payout rule, a side game.
Same
Three or more operators have the mechanic as described.
Similar
Two or more operators have it or a close relative.
Unique
Exactly one operator has it.
Family
A group of mechanics with the same purpose: draw model, fairness proof, payout and money, side games, retention and marketing, pricing and valuation.
