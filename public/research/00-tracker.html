05 → S1: what the build delivers beyond the plan

  • Everything in the plan's wave 1 except server runs for very large sizes and shared storage (both phase 2).
  • Found while building: the vendor's economy only produces a positive lifetime value when payouts are valued at retail; at realized prices with shipping, the retention lab's LTV goes negative. The presets therefore value the vendor box at retail and the Glass Box at realized, which is itself a finding for dossier 2 §3.5.
